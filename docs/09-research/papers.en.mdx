## Team work

### Flow Matching Architecture for Navigation

Poster presented at **ICRA 2025** (IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation),
Atlanta.

**Authors:** Eduardo Hernández Valdez, Alan Ulises Díaz Arenas, Arturo Eduardo Cerón López
· Tecnológico de Monterrey, Campus Monterrey, School of Engineering and Sciences.

**Poster:** [haironthecircuits.net/wiki/icra2025](https://haironthecircuits.net/wiki/icra2025/)

**Acknowledgements:** Mechatronics Department ITESM MTY, Dana Elizabeth Torres Estrada,
Dr. Luis Yoichi Morales, Dr. Luis Alberto Muñoz Ubando. Contact: `vanttec@servicios.tec.mx`

#### What it proposes

To evaluate the feasibility of a multimodal approach that generates collision-free trajectories
near-instantaneously, for robotic navigation in novel environments.

The architecture combines RGB imagery (dense input) with a 3D point cloud (sparse input, encoded
with a Scan Context++-based descriptor). Both are concatenated together with a *time embedding*
and passed through a 1D UNet with efficient channel attention (ECA) and a transformer bottleneck
with residual connections. The output is obtained by *flow matching*, integrated with the dopri5
solver.

#### Reported results

- Validated in the **PushT** environment, with a variant using RGB only and conditioning on the
  actuator position.
- Effective trajectory generation with **~40 million parameters**, against the 200-plus million
  of Diffusion Policy.
- Trained on the **SCAND** dataset.

#### Implementation

The code continuing this work is
[`FLOWMAS-ANYmal`](https://github.com/carloAdr1/FLOWMAS-ANYmal), documented in
[vision and perception](/en/docs/05-software/vision). It evolved from the poster: the second
modality moved from a sparse point cloud to **dense depth**, and the output is now a full SE(3)
trajectory.

#### Limitations the work itself acknowledges

- No unified multi-encoder able to fuse raw RGB and point cloud; today the modalities are only
  concatenated.
- No online trajectory correction during deployment; integrating online reinforcement learning
  is proposed.
- Real-world validation is pending. The planned experiment uses an EG6088K shuttle with a
  Velodyne HDL-32 and a MultiSense S21.
