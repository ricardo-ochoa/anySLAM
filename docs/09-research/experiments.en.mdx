## Carried out

### Locomotion training in Isaac Lab

**Status:** ongoing — **maintainer:** [@jesusMBhuy](https://github.com/jesusMBhuy)

Training a velocity-command-tracking policy for the ANYmal D over rough terrain, with 4096
parallel environments and up to 800 PPO iterations. Experiment identified as `Anymal_d_2` in the
configuration.

Full parameters are in [learned policies](/en/docs/05-software/learning).

<Callout type="warn" title="Results are missing">
The repository contains the configuration and a checkpoint (`policy.pt`), but no performance
metrics. Without them there is no way to know whether the policy works, nor to compare it with
future versions. Still to document: reward curve, success rate per terrain level, and whether it
has been tested on the physical robot.
</Callout>

### PushT validation

**Status:** completed — **maintainer:** [@saucesaft](https://github.com/saucesaft)

Initial validation of the flow-matching architecture in the PushT environment, using an
RGB-only variant conditioned on the actuator position. Reported in the
[ICRA 2025 poster](/en/docs/09-research/papers).

Result: effective trajectory generation with ~40 million parameters.

## How to document a new experiment

Add an entry here with this structure:

- **What is being asked** — the hypothesis, in one sentence.
- **Setup** — hardware, software, datasets, versions.
- **Method** — what was done, with which parameters.
- **Results** — numbers, not impressions.
- **Conclusion** — what it answered and what it did not.
- **Where the data is** — link to the repository or storage.

An experiment with no written results is lost the moment the person who ran it moves on.
