## Sensors in simulation (confirmed)

The training environment configures these sensors:

| Sensor | Configuration | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact forces | `ContactSensorCfg` over all bodies, 3-step history, with air-time tracking | **Active** |
| Height scanner | `RayCasterCfg` on the base, 0.1 m grid pattern over a 1.6 × 1.0 m area | **Commented out** |

The contact sensor is used in two rewards (`feet_air_time`, `undesired_contacts`) and in the
torso-contact termination.

<Callout title="The height scanner is disabled">
The `RayCasterCfg` configuration exists but is commented out in
`anymaldtraining_env_cfg.py`. That means the trained policy **receives no terrain information**:
it is a blind policy relying on proprioception alone.

This may be deliberate (blind policies are more robust to perception failures and easier to
transfer) or work in progress. Worth confirming with
[@jesusMBhuy](https://github.com/jesusMBhuy) and recording the reason here.
</Callout>

## Proprioception used by the policy

From the environment observations:

- Base linear and angular velocity — typically from an IMU on the physical robot.
- Projected gravity — orientation derived from the IMU.
- Position and velocity of the twelve joints — from encoders.
