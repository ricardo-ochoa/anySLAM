### Anymal-Research

Reinforcement-learning training of locomotion policies for the ANYmal D on NVIDIA Isaac Lab 5.0 / Isaac Sim 5.0 (PPO via RSL-RL, 4096 parallel environments, terrain curriculum, LSTM-modelled actuators), plus a ROS 2 workspace (`anymal_controller`) that deploys the trained PyTorch policy to the physical robot.

### ros2-anymal-slam

CUDA-accelerated SLAM and Monte Carlo Localization framework on ROS 2. It implements a particle filter, log-odds occupancy-grid mapping, a likelihood field, and graph SLAM with keyframes and loop closure. It includes the gRPC bridge client that translates the ANYmal's ROS 1 data into the ROS 2 `/scan` and `/odom` topics. Everything runs in Docker, with automatic platform detection (Jetson JetPack 4/5/6 or desktop) and ROS 2 distribution selection.

### ros1-anymal-client

A Docker environment with a ROS 1 client for talking to the ANYmal's ROS master without installing the full ANYbotics stack on the development machine. It ships the robot's message, service and action definitions (`anymal_interfaces`), control-lease management, keyboard teleoperation, rosbag recording and replay, and the gRPC server that streams odometry and scans to the ROS 2 side.

### ANYmal_data_management

A Tkinter application for the touchscreen of the Jetson mounted as the ANYmal payload. It orchestrates the full data chain: starting and monitoring the `ros1-anymal-client` and `ros2-anymal-slam` containers, showing the status of all three links (robot, ROS 1 bridge, ROS 2 bridge), and exposing tabs for actions, ROS data, LiDAR and cameras. It ships a simulated-data node for testing the UI without a robot.
