## What is worth settling soon

Even with the rest missing, two conventions are cheap to establish and prevent immediate clashes:

1. **One `ROS_DOMAIN_ID` per person or test bench.** Without it, two people running nodes on the
   same network see each other's topics and cause behaviour that is hard to diagnose.
2. **Write down how to reach the robot.** Address, user and access method. It is the first thing
   any new person asks.
